How does the interaction function work?
  1. We receive information through the senses.
  2. The information travels through the sensory nerves to the central nervous system.
  3. The central nervous system processes the information and produces a response.
  4. The response travels through the motor nerves to the locomotor system.
  5. We respond using our locomotor system: muscles and bones.
